## 1. Investment Thesis  

- **AI‑chip demand remains “relentless.”** Q2‑Q3 2026 data‑center GPU revenue grew > 70 % YoY, positioning NVIDIA as the de‑facto platform for generative‑AI workloads.  
- **Strategic expansion into software.** The announced acquisition/strategic investment in **Reflection AI** (video‑compression & real‑time rendering) adds a high‑margin software layer to the RTX ecosystem, widening the moat beyond pure silicon.  
- **Valuation premium is already baked in.** The $6 T market‑cap narrative reflects very high forward multiples (P/E > 80). The stock is therefore **sensitive to earnings surprises or guidance revisions**.  

**Result:** A **slightly positive** short‑term bias, contingent on the successful execution of the Reflection AI deal and a Q3 earnings beat.
