## 1. Investment Thesis  

- **AI‑centric growth engine:** AMD’s EPYC CPUs and Radeon Instinct (MI300‑series) GPUs are positioned in the fast‑expanding AI‑compute market, which IDC projects to grow > 30 % CAGR through 2030.  
- **Diversified customer base:** Beyond Microsoft Azure, AMD supplies AI accelerators to Amazon, Google, Alibaba, and a growing enterprise‑AI segment, providing a hedge against any single hyperscaler’s in‑house silicon.  
- **Technology advantage:** AMD’s chiplet architecture and 3‑D stacking deliver superior performance‑per‑watt versus competing GPUs, supporting cost‑sensitive data‑center deployments.  

*Bottom line:* The macro‑trend of exploding AI spend underpins a multi‑year revenue runway for AMD, but near‑term execution risk stems from Microsoft’s newly announced Maia‑300 AI accelerator.
